# Department Analytics Dashboard

## Project overview

Portfolio documentation for a Department Analytics dashboard completed during the DataMelon Labs Industry Project. The two-page Power BI dashboard provides a department performance overview and a category-level analysis across regions, store formats, and time periods.

## Business problem

Department and category performance need to be understood beyond total sales alone. This dashboard is designed to help stakeholders identify the departments, categories, regions, and store formats that contribute most to sales, track year-on-year growth, and compare transaction and value metrics.

## Tools and technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Department and category performance analysis

## Dashboard scope

The dashboard includes:

- Department KPIs for sales, transactions, customers, quantity, average transaction value, average selling price, units per trip, and spend per customer
- Department sales distribution and transaction metrics by region
- Rolling 12-month department performance trend
- Categories that collectively drive department sales
- Category scorecards for sales contribution, year-on-year growth, transactions, and average transaction value
- Category performance and growth comparison across Supermarket, Express, and Fresh store formats
- Selected-category sales and monthly store-format trends

## Key insights from the dashboard

- In the displayed **Beverages** department view, sales are **₹179.51M**, contributing **20.0%** of sales. The department records **544K transactions**, **1M units sold**, and **14,147 customers**.
- **North** is the largest regional contributor to Beverages sales at **₹46.6M (26.0%)**. **Central** is the smallest regional contributor shown at **₹29.2M (16.2%)**.
- **Tea & Coffee** is the largest Beverages category, contributing **60.02%** of current-year department sales and recording **27.7%** year-on-year sales growth.
- **Soft Drinks & Juices** has the strongest category sales growth in the displayed scorecard at **36.5%**, with transaction growth of **40.4%**.
- Category performance differs by store format. The total growth row shows **Fresh** at **28.8%**, **Supermarket** at **25.3%**, and **Express** at **18.2%**.
- In the selected **Health Drinks & Supplements** view, category sales are compared month by month against the prior year, and the dashboard also shows the relationship between discount percentage and monthly category sales.
